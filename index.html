devlog

01

Barangay Brawl

Godot · top-down arena fighter · concept phase

A competitive arena fighter starring creatures from Philippine folklore — Tikbalang, Aswang, Kapre, and six others, each with a full folklore-inspired ability kit. Bold flat-shaded character art with glowing per-character accents, Filipino folk-art patterns worked into the UI rather than the characters themselves.

Celebes: Abyssal Tides

Godot · 3v3 arena brawler · game jam entry

A 1-month jam entry built with a small team — a 3v3 esports-style brawler where two rival factions dive for Abyssal Pearls before an ancient sea serpent. 16-bit pixel art, kelp-zone stealth mechanics, a shrinking-map sudden death phase.
